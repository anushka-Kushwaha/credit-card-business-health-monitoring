# Credit Card Business Health Monitoring & Analytics

### An Automated Business Performance Tracking & Decision-Support System

## 1. Project Overview

Designed and developed an automated Credit Card Business Health Monitoring System to centralize daily business performance tracking across acquisition, conversion funnels, customer retention, merchandising and CRM.

The system provides a unified view of key business metrics, enabling the card team to independently monitor business health, identify performance drops and track progress against targets without depending on manual reporting.

Automated using Google Apps Script, the solution saves approximately 1–2 hours of manual reporting effort and enables continuous access to business performance insights.

## 2. Business Problem

Monitoring credit card business performance required tracking multiple metrics across different business functions and reporting sheets.

This created challenges such as:

- Limited visibility into overall daily business performance.
- Difficulty identifying drops across individual acquisition funnel stages.
- Manual effort in consolidating and updating performance reports.
- Lack of a unified view of actuals versus business targets.
- Difficulty comparing current performance with previous periods.
- Need for separate monitoring of acquisition, retention, CRM and merchandising performance.

## 3. Solution

Developed a centralized analytics and reporting framework that consolidates key business performance indicators into a structured monitoring system.

The system enables business teams to:

- Monitor daily business health through key performance indicators.
- Track acquisition and funnel progression.
- Compare actual performance against planned targets.
- Analyse customer retention across card segments.
- Evaluate in-app merchandising and CRM performance.
- Identify performance deviations for further investigation.

## 4. Key Analytics Modules

### A. Acquisition & Funnel Analytics
- Organic and referral acquisition tracking.
- Stage-wise application funnel monitoring.
- Tracking of PAN completion, application readiness, VKYC and card issuance.
- Daily acquisition performance against targets.
- Identification of funnel-stage drop-offs.

### B. Business Performance Monitoring
- Day-on-Day (DoD) performance tracking.
- Month-to-Date (MTD) performance.
- Same-Time-Last-Month (STLM) comparisons.
- Target versus actual performance.
- Daily run-rate monitoring.
- Business performance trend analysis.

### C. Customer Retention & Cohort Analytics
- RuPay and Non-RuPay customer segmentation.
- Monthly cohort-based retention tracking.
- M0–M14 cohort performance monitoring.
- Customer activity and repeat engagement analysis.

### D. Merchandising Analytics
- In-app banner-wise views and clicks.
- Click-through rate (CTR) monitoring.
- Placement-level performance comparison.
- Identification of high-performing and underperforming placements.

### E. CRM & Campaign Analytics
- Campaign-wise performance tracking.
- Customer segment-level monitoring.
- Campaign engagement and click performance.
- Comparative analysis of CRM campaigns.

## 5. Automation & Reporting Workflow

- Automated repetitive reporting processes using Google Apps Script.
- Structured reporting across multiple business tracking sheets.
- Reduced dependency on manual data consolidation.
- Improved consistency and accessibility of daily business reporting.

## 6. Tools & Skills

Tools: Google Sheets, Microsoft Excel, Google Apps Script, SQL, Metabase, Moengage

Analytics: Product Analytics, Business Performance Analysis, KPI Monitoring, Funnel Analysis, Cohort Analysis, Segmentation, Trend Analysis

Business Applications: Growth Monitoring, Acquisition Analytics, Retention Analysis, CRM Analytics, Merchandising Performance

## 7. Business Value

The project establishes a structured approach to daily business monitoring by improving:

- Visibility into overall credit card business performance.
- Identification of performance drops across critical funnel stages.
- Monitoring of business targets and growth trends.
- Understanding of customer retention and engagement.
- Evaluation of acquisition and marketing channels.
- Efficiency of recurring business reporting and reviews.

## 8. Project Objective

To build a scalable, automated and analytics-driven business monitoring framework that helps teams move from fragmented reporting to structured performance tracking and data-backed decision-making.
